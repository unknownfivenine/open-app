# OPEN

A personal, offline instrumental listening app. Open it once online and it keeps working without a
connection. Not a medical device and not clinically validated; music ending does not mean treatment
is complete. Nothing you type is saved or sent anywhere.

## Recordings

All instrument recordings are by Versilian Studios and released as CC0 (public domain):
the Versilian Community Sample Library (github.com/sgossner/VCSL) and the Chamber Orchestra 2
Community Edition (github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE), trimmed and compressed for the web.
`samples/manifest.json` lists the source file of every recording.
